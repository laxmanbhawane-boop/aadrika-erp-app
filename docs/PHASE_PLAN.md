# V2 Phase Plan

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Repository, architecture, backend shell, database, migrations, configuration, logging, health, error model, API conventions.

## Phase 1 — Identity & Organization
Company, branch, department, users, roles, permissions, fiscal year, numbering series, audit.

## Phase 2 — Master Data
Customers, suppliers, products, materials, UOM, tax, warehouses, locations, machines, assets, employees.

## Phase 3 — Commercial
CRM, quotation, universal costing engine, Aadrika bag costing, sales order.

## Phase 4 — Supply Chain
Purchase requisition, RFQ, supplier comparison, PO, GRN, inventory ledger, warehouse.

## Phase 5 — Manufacturing
BOM, routing, MRP, production planning, production order, work orders, WIP, finished goods.

## Phase 6 — Industry
Printing, artwork, prepress, ink, make-ready, packaging, finishing, handle management, scrap/recovery.

## Phase 7 — Quality & Assets
QC, NCR, CAPA, machine maintenance, fixed assets, tools, spares.

## Phase 8 — Logistics & Finance
Dispatch, invoice, AR/AP, GL, banking, GST, expenses, profitability.

## Phase 9 — Intelligence
MIS, dashboards, analytics, alerts, forecasting and AI.

## Phase 10 — Smart Factory
Barcode/QR, OEE, machine integration, weighing, IoT and predictive maintenance.
